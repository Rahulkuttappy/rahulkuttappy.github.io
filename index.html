I FILM WHAT LINGERS

Not the event, the second after. Not the word, the breath before it.

I'm Rahul, a filmmaker and editor from Calicut, currently based in Bengaluru. I started with a camera because I couldn't stop watching films and needed to know what it felt like from the other side.

I work with brands, musicians, and people with something real to say, from fashion and automobiles to music videos and documentary. My process is deliberate and my cuts are earned.

I'm not interested in footage. I'm interested in feeling.
