The Brief

SHE
IS YOU

Knari's SS'24 line needed a film that sold the drape of the fabric more than the styling around it. Movement first, product second, and the tagline carrying the rest. Cut from the footage as shot.

We kept the palette tight and the camera patient. Slow push-ins, a locked frame where the garment does the moving, and negative space wherever the fabric needed room.

The whole campaign rests on one line: she is you. Everything in the cut serves that idea.
