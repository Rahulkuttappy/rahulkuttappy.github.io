A series of trailers cut for Miles Masterclass, each introducing a different speaker and a different subject, all sharing one visual grammar.

The challenge with talking-head source material is pace. Every trailer had to establish authority in the first three seconds and land a hook before the thirty second mark.

Same system, three tempos. Joe runs analytical, Alex runs quick, Dirk runs cinematic, and the grade holds them together as one family.
