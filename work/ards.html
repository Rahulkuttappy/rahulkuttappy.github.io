ARDS

ClientARDS
Year2024
RoleCinematography, Edit
FormatPerformance Night

The Brief

EVERY STYLE,
ONE NIGHT

ARDS is a street dance studio in Bengaluru. Once a year the classes come off the studio floor and onto a stage, and the students perform for a room full of people who have watched them train. I shot their performance night end to end.

The night runs category by category, so the film changes shape every few minutes. Each style arrives with its own music, its own attitude and its own way of holding the floor, and the coverage has to switch with it rather than settle into one rhythm.

There are no second takes at a live show. The approach was to shoot it like a concert rather than a recital: long lenses, wide apertures, and a willingness to let the haze and the spill do the work. Where the lighting went hard red or hard blue, I leaned into it instead of correcting it out.

In the edit, the cut follows breath rather than beat. The pauses carry as much as the movement. Grading kept the blacks genuinely black so the colour that survives feels earned, and the result looks like the room actually felt: hot, loud, and briefly weightless.
