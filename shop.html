Before You Buy

HOW THESE
ARE MEANT
TO BE USED

01

BALANCE FIRST

Set exposure and white balance before the LUT goes anywhere near the clip. A LUT applied to an unbalanced shot will look wrong, and it will not be the LUT's fault.

02

DROP IT ON AN ADJUSTMENT LAYER

Keep it off the clips themselves. You want to be able to pull the whole look back by one number when the client asks for less.

03

THEN DIAL IT BACK

Most of these sit best somewhere between sixty and eighty percent. Full strength is a starting position, not the intended result.

04

QUESTIONS

If something does not behave on your footage, mail me. I would rather fix the pack than have it sitting unused in your downloads folder.
