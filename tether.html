TETHER

Motion-seeking FUI overlays, drawn out of the footage itself. Tether finds what matters in a shot and builds the interface around it.

Runs InDaVinci Resolve
TypeFuse, Fusion page
Looks10 built in
StatusComing soon

What It Does

IT WATCHES,
THEN IT DRAWS

Point Tether at a shot and it goes looking. It picks its subject out by key colour, by motion, by luminance or by alpha, then draws the overlay around whatever it found and keeps it there as the shot moves.

Everything it draws is tied to the frame underneath: boxes that hold onto the subject, lines that run between them, readouts that change as the numbers change.

No keyframing an interface by hand. The footage does the tracking, and you spend your time on how it looks instead of where it sits.
